How this estimate works

Token estimate: characters divided by 4 (a rough average for English + code; a real tokenizer will differ, usually by less than 20%). Cost estimate: $3 per million input tokens, a typical mid-tier model list price at the time of writing - check your provider's current pricing. Prompt caching can reduce repeat-read cost substantially; this shows the uncached worst case. The context-window figure assumes a 200k window.
