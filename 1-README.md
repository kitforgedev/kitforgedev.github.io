# KitForge

Small free tools for people who work with AI coding agents. Everything runs as static pages in your browser: no accounts, no uploads, no tracking.

## Tools

- **[CLAUDE.md cost checker](https://kitforgedev.github.io/claude-md-cost/)** - estimate how many tokens your CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md adds to every session, priced against current Claude list prices.
- **[AI rules sanity check](https://kitforgedev.github.io/rules-check/)** - structural checks for CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, and Cursor .mdc rules. Heuristic flags are review prompts, not verdicts.

## Research

- **[What 12 real CLAUDE.md files from major repos look like](https://kitforgedev.github.io/claude-md-survey/)** - a measured survey of root CLAUDE.md files at pinned commits (React, Electron, PyTorch, Bun, and 8 more), with sample selection, method, and caveats. Also published on [Hashnode](https://kitforgedev.hashnode.dev/what-12-real-claude-md-files-from-major-repos-look-like).

## Kit

- **[Agentic Coding Kit](https://kitforgedev.itch.io/agentic-coding-kit)** - templates, presets, hooks, and CI checks for agent coding workflows. A free sampler is on the itch page.
